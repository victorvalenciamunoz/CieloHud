# Paloma

La paloma de Noé, que volvió al arca con una rama de olivo cuando el diluvio empezaba a bajar. La formó a finales del
siglo XVI el holandés Petrus Plancius, con estrellas que Ptolomeo había dejado fuera del Can Mayor. Vuela detrás del
Argo, el barco de Jasón, y quien conozca su historia puede verla como la paloma que soltaron los argonautas para pasar
entre las Rocas Chocantes.

## Fuentes

- Ian Ridpath, Star Tales, Columba (ianridpath.com/startales/columba.html): «A constellation formed in the late 16th century by the Dutch cartographer and astronomer Petrus Plancius, who took some stars that Ptolemy in his Almagest had catalogued as lying outside Canis Major»; «Columba is supposed to represent Noah's dove, sent out from the Ark to find dry land, and which returned with an olive branch in its beak, a sign that the Flood was at last subsiding»; «The dove flies behind Argo Navis, the ship»; «those familiar with the story of Argo might instead think of Columba as the dove sent by the Argonauts between the sliding doors of the Clashing Rocks to ensure their safe passage».
