# Copa

La copa que Apolo dio al cuervo para que le trajera agua de un manantial. En la antigua Grecia, una crátera no era una copa
como las nuestras, sino un recipiente para mezclar el vino con agua. Está sobre el lomo de la Hidra, inclinada hacia el
Cuervo pero justo fuera de su alcance: es parte de su castigo.

## Fuentes

- Ian Ridpath, Star Tales, Corvus and Crater (ianridpath.com/startales/corvusandcrater.html): «Apollo was about to make a sacrifice to Zeus and sent the crow to fetch water from a running spring. The crow flew off with a bowl in its claws»; «In ancient Greece, a krater was a bowl or vase used for mixing wine with water, rather than a cup as we know it»; «Corvus and Crater, two adjacent constellations on the back of Hydra»; «The cup [...] is shown tilted towards the crow but tantalizingly just out of the thirsty bird's reach»; «condemned the crow to a life of thirst».
