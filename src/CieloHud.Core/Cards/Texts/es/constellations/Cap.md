# Capricornio

Una cabra con cola de pez, de origen sumerio. Los griegos la identificaron con Pan, el dios con cuernos y patas
de cabra: huyendo del monstruo Tifón, se echó a un río y la parte inferior de su cuerpo se volvió de pez. De su grito, que
asustaba a la gente, viene «pánico». En tiempos de los griegos, el Sol estaba en ella en el solsticio de diciembre: de ahí
el nombre del trópico de Capricornio.

## Fuentes

- Ian Ridpath, Star Tales, Capricornus (ianridpath.com/startales/capricornus.html): «Capricornus is an unlikely looking creature, with the head and forelegs of a goat and the tail of a fish. The constellation evidently originated with the Sumerians and Babylonians»; «to the Greeks [...] the constellation was identified with Pan, god of the countryside, who had the horns and legs of a goat»; «He could frighten people with his loud shout, which is the origin of the word 'panic'»; «Pan himself took refuge in a river, turning the lower part of his body into a fish»; «The tropic of Capricorn is the latitude on Earth at which the Sun appears overhead at noon on the winter solstice, around December 22. In Greek times the Sun was in Capricornus on this date».
