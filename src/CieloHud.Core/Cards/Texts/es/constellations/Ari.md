# Aries

El carnero alado del vellocino de oro. Bajó del cielo para salvar a Frixo, al que su madrastra había conseguido que
fueran a sacrificar, y se lo llevó volando con su hermana Hele; Hele se cayó por el camino al estrecho que los griegos
llamaron Helesponto. Su vellocino fue lo que fueron a buscar Jasón y los argonautas. Se reconoce por una línea torcida de
tres estrellas, la cabeza.

## Fuentes

- Ian Ridpath, Star Tales, Aries (ianridpath.com/startales/aries.html): «the mythographers agree that Aries is a special ram, the one whose golden fleece was the object of the voyage of Jason and the Argonauts»; «Ino resented her step-children, Phrixus and Helle, and she arranged a plot to have them killed»; «Ino bribed messengers to bring back a false reply that Phrixus must be sacrificed»; «Nephele intervened to save her son, sending down from the sky a winged ram with a golden fleece. Phrixus climbed on the ram's back and was joined by his sister Helle»; «On the way Helle's grip failed and she fell into the channel between Europe and Asia, the Dardanelles, which the Greeks named the Hellespont in her memory»; «Its most noticeable feature is a crooked line of three stars, which marks its head».
