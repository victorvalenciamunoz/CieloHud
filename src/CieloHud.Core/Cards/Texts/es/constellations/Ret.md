# Retículo

La retícula del ocular del pequeño telescopio con el que Lacaille catalogó las estrellas del sur desde el cabo de Buena
Esperanza: un rombo recortado en una placa de cobre que le servía para medir sus posiciones cuando cruzaban el campo de
visión. Lo puso junto al Reloj con el que cronometraba esos cruces. Sustituyó a una figura anterior, el Rombo, de 1621.

## Fuentes

- Ian Ridpath, Star Tales, Reticulum (ianridpath.com/startales/reticulum.html): «introduced by the French astronomer Nicolas Louis de Lacaille to commemorate the reticle in the eyepiece of his small telescope with which he catalogued the southern stars from the Cape of Good Hope in 1751–52. The reticle consisted of a diamond-shaped aperture cut from a copper plate inserted into the eyepiece which helped him measure the position of stars as they passed through the field of view»; «In the sky it lies conveniently next to Lacaille's observatory clock, Horologium, which he used for timing the passage of stars through the reticle»; «Lacaille's Reticulum replaced a previous constellation in this area called Rhombus that was introduced in 1621».
