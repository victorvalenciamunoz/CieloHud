# Can Menor

El perro pequeño de Orión; al principio, la constelación era solo Proción. En una leyenda de Atenas es Mera, el perro de
Icario, el primer hombre al que Dioniso enseñó a hacer vino: unos pastores se emborracharon con él, creyeron que los había
envenenado y lo mataron. Mera llevó a la hija de Icario, Erígone, hasta el cuerpo. En el cielo, Icario es el Boyero, y
Erígone, Virgo.

## Fuentes

- Ian Ridpath, Star Tales, Canis Minor (ianridpath.com/startales/canisminor.html): «Representing the smaller of the two dogs of Orion, Canis Minor originally consisted of just the bright star Procyon»; «in a famous legend from Attica (the area around Athens), recounted by the mythographer Hyginus, the constellation represents Maera, dog of Icarius, the man whom the god Dionysus first taught to make wine. When Icarius gave his wine to some shepherds for tasting, they rapidly became drunk. Suspecting that Icarius had poisoned them, they killed him. Maera the dog ran howling to Icarius's daughter Erigone [...] and led her to her father's body»; «In this story, Icarius is identified with the constellation Boötes, Erigone is Virgo, and Maera is Canis Minor».
