# Cincel

Una herramienta de grabador, otra invención de Lacaille, de 1756. En realidad eran dos, cruzadas y atadas con una cinta:
un buril y una «échoppe», una aguja de grabar que inventó el grabador francés Jacques Callot en el siglo XVII. En 1844, John
Herschel propuso acortar su nombre a una sola palabra. No tiene leyendas.

## Fuentes

- Ian Ridpath, Star Tales, Caelum (ianridpath.com/startales/caelum.html): «representing an engraver's chisel, is one of the inventions of the 18th-century French astronomer Nicolas Louis de Lacaille. He introduced it on his map of the southern stars published in 1756»; «In his notes to the 1756 chart Lacaille said that the constellation represented two engraving tools, crossed and connected by a ribbon. One tool was a burin [...] while the other was an échoppe, a type of etching needle invented by the 17th-century French printmaker Jacques Callot»; «In 1844 the English astronomer John Herschel proposed shortening the name to Caelum»; «There are no legends associated with the constellation».
