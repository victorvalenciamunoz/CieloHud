# Corona Boreal

La corona de oro de Ariadna, la princesa de Creta que dio a Teseo el ovillo de hilo para salir del laberinto del Minotauro.
Teseo la abandonó en la isla de Naxos, y allí se casó con ella el dios Dioniso, que lanzó la corona al cielo. En ella está
la Estrella Llameante, una nova recurrente: en 1866 se encendió de golpe hasta verse a simple vista, y volvió a hacerlo en
1946.

## Fuentes

- Ian Ridpath, Star Tales, Corona Borealis (ianridpath.com/startales/coronaborealis.html): «A semicircle of stars between Boötes and Hercules marks the golden crown worn by Princess Ariadne of Crete when she married the god Dionysus»; «Daedalus, who gave her a ball of thread and advised Theseus to tie one end to the door of the labyrinth»; «no sooner had they reached the island of Naxos than he abandoned her»; «Dionysus joyfully tossed the crown into the sky where its jewels transformed into stars»; «In 1866 the Irish astronomer John Birmingham discovered a 'new' star of magnitude 2 [...] which faded below naked-eye visibility over the following week [...] It turned out to be a recurrent nova, having flared up again in 1946»; «The British spectroscopist William Allen Miller referred to it as the Blaze Star».
