# Centauro

Quirón, el centauro sabio, maestro de Jasón, de Aquiles y de Asclepio, el gran sanador. En una de sus patas delanteras
está Alfa Centauri, la estrella más cercana al Sol: a simple vista es la tercera más brillante del cielo, y con un
telescopio pequeño se ve que son dos, una casi idéntica al Sol. Se va acercando en el cielo a Hadar, la Beta: dentro de
unos 4000 años parecerán casi juntas.

## Fuentes

- Ian Ridpath, Star Tales, Centaurus (ianridpath.com/startales/centaurus.html): «one centaur, Chiron, stood out from the rest as being wise and scholarly, and he is the one who is represented by the constellation Centaurus»; «he was made foster-father to Jason (of Argonauts fame) and Achilles; but perhaps his most successful pupil was Asclepius, son of Apollo, who became the greatest of all healers»; «Alpha Centauri, the closest star to the Sun, marks the centaur's forefoot»; «Beta Centauri is called Hadar»; «Alpha Centauri has one of the largest proper motions of any naked-eye star, and this motion is gradually carrying it towards Beta Centauri as seen from Earth»; «around 4,000 years from now they will be little more than ½° (one Moon diameter) apart in our skies».
- Jim Kaler, Stars, Rigil Kentaurus (stars.astro.illinois.edu/sow/rigil.html): «the third brightest star in the sky (after Sirius and Canopus)»; «it is the closest star to the Sun»; «Through but a modest telescope we see it as double. The brighter is a yellow class G dwarf (hydrogen-fusing) star that [...] appears almost identical to the Sun».
