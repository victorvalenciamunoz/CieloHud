# Virgo

Dice, la diosa de la justicia, que vivió entre los humanos en la Edad de Oro. Cuando se volvieron peores, los abandonó y
subió al cielo; allí está, junto a Libra, que algunos ven como la balanza de la justicia.
Otros la identificaban con Perséfone, que pasa parte del año en el inframundo: un relato de las estaciones. En Virgo está
el punto donde el Sol cruza el ecuador del cielo en septiembre.

## Fuentes

- Ian Ridpath, Star Tales, Virgo (ianridpath.com/startales/virgo.html): «She is usually identified as Dike, goddess of justice»; «Dike was supposed to have lived on Earth in the Golden Age of mankind»; «Then she spread her wings and took refuge in the mountains, turning her back on mankind [...] Unable to endure the sins of humanity any longer, Dike abandoned the Earth and flew up to heaven, where she sits to this day next to the constellation of Libra, which some see as the scales of justice»; «Persephone would spend half (some say one-third) of the year in the Underworld [...] Clearly, this is an allegory on the changing seasons»; «Virgo, incidentally, contains the autumnal equinox, the point at which the Sun crosses the celestial equator heading south; this occurs on September 22 or 23 each year».
