# Dorado

No es un pez de colores: es el dorado o mahi-mahi de los mares tropicales, que los navegantes holandeses veían cazar
peces voladores; por eso persigue al Pez Volador. En él está casi toda la Gran Nube de Magallanes, una pequeña galaxia
vecina de la nuestra, con la nebulosa de la Tarántula dentro. Américo Vespucio ya la describió en 1502, antes del viaje de
Magallanes.

## Fuentes

- Ian Ridpath, Star Tales, Dorado (ianridpath.com/startales/dorado.html): «The constellation represents the colourful dolphinfish Coryphaena hippurus (also known as mahi-mahi) found in tropical waters, not the goldfish commonly found in ponds and aquaria. Dutch explorers observed these large predatory fish chasing flying fish and so Dorado was placed in the sky following the constellation of the flying fish, Volans»; «Dorado's main claim to fame is that it contains most of the Large Magellanic Cloud, a small neighbour galaxy of our own Milky Way»; «first described by the Italian explorer Amerigo Vespucci (1454–1512) in an account written in 1502»; «Antonio Pigafetta [...] who sailed with Ferdinand Magellan on the first circumnavigation of the globe in 1519–22, wrote of 'two clouds'»; «Within the Large Magellanic Cloud lies the huge nebula NGC 2070, popularly called the Tarantula».
