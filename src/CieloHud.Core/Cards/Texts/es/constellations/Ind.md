# Indio

Un indígena con una lanza, como si estuviera cazando: otra figura de los navegantes holandeses Keyser y de Houtman. No se
sabe a quién representa: quizá a un nativo de Madagascar, donde la flota pasó meses y tomó muchas de sus observaciones, o
a todos los pueblos que encontraron en sus viajes. Los primeros dibujos se parecen a un grabado de un malgache de 1598.

## Fuentes

- Ian Ridpath, Star Tales, Indus (ianridpath.com/startales/indus.html): «Indus is one of the 12 figures formed by the Dutch navigators Pieter Dirkszoon Keyser and Frederick de Houtman»; «The Indian is portrayed brandishing a spear as though hunting. Whether he is supposed to be a native of Madagascar, where the Dutch fleet stayed for several months on their way east and made many of their astronomical observations, is not known»; «Or perhaps the figure is symbolic, representing all the indigenous peoples the explorers encountered on their various travels»; «early depictions such as on Plancius's globe and in Bayer's Uranometria strongly resemble this engraving of a native of Madagascar from an account of the first Dutch voyage to the East Indies [...] first published in 1598».
