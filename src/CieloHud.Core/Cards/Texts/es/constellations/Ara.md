# Altar

El altar en el que los dioses, con Zeus al frente, juraron derrotar a los titanes; tras diez años de guerra, Zeus lo puso
en el cielo en agradecimiento. Su nombre griego era el de un incensario, y se suele dibujar con las llamas hacia el sur.
En otros mapas es el altar en el que Centauro va a sacrificar al Lobo.

## Fuentes

- Ian Ridpath, Star Tales, Ara (ianridpath.com/startales/ara.html): «this altar is a special one, for it was used by the gods themselves to swear a vow of allegiance before their fight against the Titans»; «Zeus and his brother gods then set up an altar and vowed on it to overthrow the callous rule of Cronus and the other Titans»; «The battle raged ten years»; «Zeus then placed the altar of the gods in the sky as the constellation Ara in lasting gratitude for their victory over the Titans»; «The Greek name for the constellation was θυμιατήριον (Thymiaterion), referring to an incense burner or censer»; «The altar is usually depicted with its base to the north and its flames rising southwards»; «Other atlases depict Ara as the altar on which Centaurus is about to sacrifice Lupus, the wolf».
