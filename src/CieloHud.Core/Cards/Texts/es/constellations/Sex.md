# Sextante

El sextante con el que Hevelius medía a simple vista la posición de las estrellas, desde el observatorio que tenía en el
tejado de su casa de Danzig. Un incendio lo destruyó en 1679, y en 1687 lo puso en el cielo. Quizá la formó con estrellas
tan débiles para demostrar la vista que tenía, como hizo con el Lince.

## Fuentes

- Ian Ridpath, Star Tales, Sextans (ianridpath.com/startales/sextans.html): «introduced by the Polish astronomer Johannes Hevelius in his star catalogue of 1687 [...] It commemorated the instrument he used for measuring star positions, which was destroyed along with other instruments in a fire at his home in 1679»; «his rooftop observatory, which was built over three adjoining properties he owned in [...] Danzig (the modern Gdańsk)»; «Hevelius had continued to make naked-eye sightings with his sextant throughout his life»; «it was perhaps to demonstrate the keenness of his eyes that he formed Sextans out of such faint stars, as he also did with another of his inventions, Lynx».
