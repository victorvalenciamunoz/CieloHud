# Leo

El león de Nemea, el primero de los doce trabajos de Hércules: ninguna arma atravesaba su piel, así que lo estranguló y
después se vistió con ella. Es fácil ver en sus estrellas un león agazapado, con la cabeza y el pecho dibujados por seis
estrellas en forma de hoz. En China, esas estrellas eran parte de un dragón amarillo, la imagen del Emperador Amarillo,
fundador legendario de su civilización.

## Fuentes

- Ian Ridpath, Star Tales, Leo (ianridpath.com/startales/leo.html): «this is reputed to be the lion of Nemea, slain by Heracles as the first of his 12 labours»; «Its skin was proof against all weapons»; «locking his huge arm around its throat and choking the beast to death»; «he used the creature's own razor-sharp claws to cut off its pelt, which he wore as a cloak»; «it is easy to make out the shape of a crouching lion among its stars. The head and chest are outlined by six stars arranged in the shape of a sickle»; «On Chinese star charts the Sickle of Leo is recognizable, but as part of a rather different constellation figure»; «the whole formation was known as Xuanyuan, the Yellow Dragon»; «a legendary ruler who is credited with being the main founder of Chinese civilization; the Yellow Dragon (Xuanyuan) snaking among the other stars of Leo was said to be his image immortalized in the sky».
