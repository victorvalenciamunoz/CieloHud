# Can Mayor

El perro guardián de Orión, que va tras su amo; seguramente la constelación nació solo con Sirio, la estrella del Perro.
Los mitógrafos lo identificaban con Lélape, un perro al que no se le escapaba ninguna presa, lanzado tras una zorra a la
que nadie podía atrapar. Como aquella persecución no podía acabar, Zeus convirtió a los dos en piedra y puso al perro en
el cielo, sin la zorra.

## Fuentes

- Ian Ridpath, Star Tales, Canis Major (ianridpath.com/startales/canismajor.html): «Aratus referred to Canis Major as the guard-dog of Orion, following on the heels of its master»; «Canis Major is dominated by Sirius, popularly termed the Dog Star [...] almost certainly the constellation originated with this star alone»; «Mythologists such as Eratosthenes and Hyginus said that the constellation represented Laelaps, a dog so swift that no prey could outrun it»; «The fox was so swift of foot that it was destined never to be caught – yet Laelaps the hound was destined to catch whatever it pursued»; «There could be no resolution of such a paradox, so Zeus turned them both to stone, and the dog he placed in the sky as Canis Major, without the fox».
