# Leo Menor

Un cachorro de león junto a Leo, que inventó Hevelius en 1687 con estrellas débiles entre la Osa Mayor y Leo. No tiene
leyendas, y tampoco estrella Alfa, por un descuido: cuando Francis Baily puso letras griegas a sus estrellas en 1845, dio
la Beta a la segunda más brillante y se olvidó de poner letra a la primera.

## Fuentes

- Ian Ridpath, Star Tales, Leo Minor (ianridpath.com/startales/leominor.html): «A lion cub accompanying Leo, introduced by the Polish astronomer Johannes Hevelius in his star catalogue of 1687»; «He formed it from 18 faint stars between Ursa Major and Leo»; «there are no legends associated with it»; «Leo Minor has no star labelled Alpha [...] This seems to have resulted from an oversight on the part of the 19th-century English astronomer Francis Baily [...] In his British Association Catalogue of 1845, Baily assigned the letter Beta to the second-brightest star in Leo Minor, but left the brightest star [...] unlettered by mistake».
