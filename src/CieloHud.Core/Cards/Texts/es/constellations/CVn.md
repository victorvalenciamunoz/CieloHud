# Perros de Caza

Dos lebreles, Asterión y Chara, que el Boyero lleva atados y que van tras la Osa Mayor. Los formó Hevelius en 1687 con
estrellas débiles bajo la cola de la Osa, aunque Blaeu ya había dibujado allí dos perros en 1602. En ellos está la galaxia
del Remolino: en 1845, Lord Rosse vio en ella por primera vez la forma de espiral de una galaxia.

## Fuentes

- Ian Ridpath, Star Tales, Canes Venatici (ianridpath.com/startales/canesvenatici.html): «The Polish astronomer Johannes Hevelius formed this constellation in 1687 from a scattering of faint stars beneath the tail of Ursa Major. Canes Venatici represents a pair of greyhounds held on a lead by Boötes, snapping at the heels of the Great Bear. Hevelius named the dogs Asterion ('Starry') and Chara ('Dear')»; «in 1602 the Dutch cartographer Willem Janszoon Blaeu [...] produced a globe in which Boötes holds two dogs that are following the Great Bear [...] Blaeu deserves at least a share of the credit for the invention of Canes Venatici»; «Canes Venatici contains a beautiful spiral galaxy, M51, popularly called the Whirlpool. M51 was the first galaxy in which spiral form was noticed, by the Irish astronomer Lord Rosse (1800–67) in 1845».
