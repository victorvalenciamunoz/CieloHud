# Ofiuco

Asclepio, el gran médico, que aprendió del centauro Quirón y llegó a resucitar a los muertos. Hades se quejó de que se
quedaba sin almas, y Zeus lo fulminó con un rayo, aunque después lo puso en el cielo. El Sol pasa por Ofiuco en la primera
mitad de diciembre, aunque no es del zodiaco. En 1604 apareció en su tobillo la última supernova vista en nuestra galaxia,
la estrella de Kepler.

## Fuentes

- Ian Ridpath, Star Tales, Ophiuchus (ianridpath.com/startales/ophiuchus.html): «The Greeks identified him as Asclepius, the god of medicine»; «Chiron raised Asclepius as his own son, teaching him the arts of healing and hunting. Asclepius became so skilled in medicine that not only could he save lives, he could also raise the dead»; «Hades, god of the Underworld, began to realize that the flow of dead souls into his domain would soon dry up [...] He complained to his brother god Zeus who struck down Asclepius with a thunderbolt»; «Zeus made Asclepius immortal [...] and set him among the stars as the constellation Ophiuchus»; «Although Ophiuchus is not one of the official 12 constellations of the zodiac, the Sun passes through its southern regions in the first half of December»; «Ophiuchus was the site of the last supernova seen in our Galaxy. This appeared in 1604 near Xi Ophiuchi in the right ankle of Ophiuchus [...] It is known as Kepler's Star».
