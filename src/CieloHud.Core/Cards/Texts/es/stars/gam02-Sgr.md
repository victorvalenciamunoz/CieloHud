# Alnasl

Su nombre árabe significa «la punta»: es la de la flecha de Sagitario. Si sigues la flecha hacia el oeste otro tanto,
llegas casi al centro de nuestra galaxia, oculto tras el polvo, donde casi seguro hay un enorme agujero negro. Cerca está
Gamma 1, una estrella que cambia de brillo de forma muy clara cada 7,59 días.

## Fuentes

- Jim Kaler, Stars, Alnasl (stars.astro.illinois.edu/sow/alnasl.html): «The bright one you readily see makes the tip of Sagittarius's arrow, the name coming directly from the Arabic phrase that means "the point."»; «follow Sagittarius's arrow to the west by a distance about equal to its length ..., and then look up a degree and a half to locate the center of our Galaxy»; «The Galactic center, so buried in thick interstellar dust that it is not visible optically, is almost universally believed to be a massive black hole»; «Less than a degree to the north is fifth magnitude (4.7) Gamma-1 ... A Cepheid variable ... regularly and obviously varies between magnitudes 4.3 and 5.1 over a 7.59 day period».
