# Caph

Las cinco estrellas de la W de Casiopea eran para los árabes los dedos de una mano abierta, y de ahí su nombre: «la mano
teñida», con henna. Al final se lo quedó solo esta. Es la estrella más brillante de un tipo de variables que laten deprisa:
su brillo cambia un 6 % cada dos horas y media.

## Fuentes

- Jim Kaler, Stars, Caph (stars.astro.illinois.edu/sow/caph.html): «The five stars of the "W" splay out like the fingers of a stretched hand, the name "Caph" coming from an Arabic phrase meaning "the stained hand," which refers to an ancient cosmetic coloring by henna. The name eventually went to the Beta star»; «Caph is also unusual in being a "Delta Scuti" type of variable star, in fact the brightest of the breed. Slightly unstable, and rapidly pulsating»; «the star varies by about 6 percent in brightness over a 2.5 hour period».
