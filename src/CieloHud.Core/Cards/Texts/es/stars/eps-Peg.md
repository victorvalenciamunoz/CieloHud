# Enif

La más brillante de Pegaso; su nombre árabe significa «la nariz», la del hocico del caballo. Es una supergigante que
probablemente nació a la vez que Sadalmelik y Sadalsuud, de Acuario, y las tres se han ido separando. En 1972, un observador
la vio brillar de repente como Altair durante más de diez minutos: uno de los fogonazos más raros de las estrellas.

## Fuentes

- Jim Kaler, Stars, Enif (stars.astro.illinois.edu/sow/enif.html): «Enif ... is still the brightest star in the constellation Pegasus»; «The name, from Arabic, means "the nose," referring to the muzzle of Perseus' winged horse»; «a coolish, orange class K (K2) supergiant»; «It seems to be part of a family of three very similar supergiants, the other two the Alpha and Beta stars of nearby Aquarius ... may have been born together in the same extended group, and over the past 15 or so million years of their existence have drifted well over 100 light years apart»; «In 1972, an observer in Florida saw Enif as bright as Altair in Aquila, five times brighter than normal ... For over 10 minutes it appeared to pop some kind of enormous flare ... Such events are rare».
