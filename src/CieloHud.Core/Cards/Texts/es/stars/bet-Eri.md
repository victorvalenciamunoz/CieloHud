# Cursa

Su nombre árabe significa «el reposapiés delantero» de Orión: un grupo de cuatro estrellas junto a Rigel, donde el gigante
apoya el pie. Es el comienzo de Erídano, el río del cielo. En 1985 se la vio multiplicar su brillo por 15 durante más de
dos horas: uno de los fogonazos más raros y misteriosos de las estrellas.

## Fuentes

- Jim Kaler, Stars, Cursa (stars.astro.illinois.edu/sow/cursa.html): «The "foremost footstool" consists of a four-star box just up and to the right of Rigel, which marks Orion's left foot. Of these, Cursa ... is notably the brightest, and as a result took on the name of the whole "footstool," "Cursa" from an Arabic phrase meaning "the foremost footstool of the Central One," the ancient Arabic name for our Orion»; «Cursa begins the River Eridanus, the celestial depiction of the Greek's "Ocean Stream"»; «In 1985 it was observed to brighten by a phenomenal three magnitudes (a factor of 15) for a period of over two hours»; «they are among the great mysteries of stellar astronomy».
