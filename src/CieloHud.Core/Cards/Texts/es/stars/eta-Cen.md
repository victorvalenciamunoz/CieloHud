# Eta de Centauro

Forma parte de una enorme asociación de estrellas jóvenes y calientes, nacidas a la vez, que ocupa Escorpio, Centauro y el
Lobo y se va dispersando. Gira en menos de un día, y está rodeada de un disco de gas que vemos de canto, de modo que tapa
parte de la estrella.

## Fuentes

- Jim Kaler, Stars, Eta Cen (stars.astro.illinois.edu/sow/etacen.html): «Eta is a very hot class B (B1.5) dwarf that is part of the sprawling Scorpius-Centaurus (Sco-Cen) "OB association," ... Eta belongs to the "Upper Centaurus-Lupus" (UCL) group. Such associations are loose collections of stars born more or less at the same time ... they are slowly expanding»; «it rotates quickly with an equatorial speed of at least 310 kilometers per second, giving it a rotation period of less than a day»; «a thick circumstellar disk ... Eta is of an extreme sort called a "shell star," as the disk is rather edge-on to the line of sight».
- Jim Kaler, Stars, Alpha Ara (stars.astro.illinois.edu/sow/alphaara.html): de las «shell stars», «the disk is nearly edge on, obscures part of the star».
