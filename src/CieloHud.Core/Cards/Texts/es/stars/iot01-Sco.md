# Iota 1 de Escorpio

Una supergigante de un tipo poco común, ni roja ni azul, en plena transformación hacia supergigante roja. A su lado se ve
Iota 2, otra supergigante, pero el parecido es casual: está a casi el doble de distancia. Es buena candidata a estallar
algún día como supernova.

## Fuentes

- Jim Kaler, Stars, Iota-1 Sco (stars.astro.illinois.edu/sow/iota1sco.html): «Iota-1 is a mid-third magnitude (3.02) yellow-white class F (F2) supergiant that visually dominates its fourth magnitude (4.81) neighbor, the class A (A2) supergiant Iota-2. The alignment is only accidental, however, Iota-2 roughly double Iota-1's distance»; «Most supergiants are red, or at least hot and blue. Even at 20 solar masses, "yellow" supergiants such as this one are quite rare, and are in a state of evolutionary transition in which ... they are in the process of becoming red supergiants»; «the star is a good candidate for exploding as a supernova».
