# Albaldah

Su nombre viene de una de las 28 «casas» de la Luna de los árabes, las paradas de la Luna en su camino por el cielo: la
número 21, «la ciudad», que suele entenderse como La Meca. Es una gigante a punto de empezar a latir como las cefeidas, las
estrellas que cambian de brillo como un reloj: lo hará dentro de unos 1,5 millones de años.

## Fuentes

- Jim Kaler, Stars, Albaldah (stars.astro.illinois.edu/sow/albaldah.html): «Arabian star lore established 28 "manzils," or "daily stations," or "mansions" for the Moon in its path along the ecliptic. Number 21, "Albaldah" or "Al Baldah," gave its name to the star we now call "Pi Sagittarii," and refers to "the City," in fact taken commonly as referring to Makkah, or Mecca»; «an impressive class F (F2) "bright giant"»; «the star is on the "blue edge" of the "Cepheid instability strip," the zone where stars ... pulsate and change their brightnesses like well-oiled clocks. Albaldah should become one of their number in about 1.5 million years».
