# Wezen

Su nombre árabe significa «peso», y le va bien: es de las estrellas más pesadas que se ven a simple vista, con unas 17 veces
la masa del Sol. Es una supergigante tan grande que en el lugar del Sol casi llegaría a la órbita de la Tierra. Tiene solo
unos 10 millones de años y acabará estallando como supernova.

## Fuentes

- Jim Kaler, Stars, Wezen (stars.astro.illinois.edu/sow/wezen.html): «the name literally means, from Arabic, "weight," as in heavy»; «Oddly, "weight" is a wonderful name for this magnificent star, as it is indeed "weighty," as in massive; it is certainly one of the more massive that grace the naked-eye nightly sky»; «Wezen weighs in at about 17 solar masses»; «a full-blown yellow supergiant»; «a supergiant radius 195 times that of the Sun, which makes the star almost as big as the Earth's orbit»; «It is only about 10 million years old»; «a brilliant explosion, a supernova».
