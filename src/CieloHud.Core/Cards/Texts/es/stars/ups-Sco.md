# Lesath

Con Shaula forma el aguijón de Escorpio, aunque no son una pareja real. Su nombre es un lío de tres idiomas: venía de una
palabra griega que significa «neblinoso», seguramente por el cúmulo de estrellas M7, que está cerca, y después de pasar por
el árabe y el latín acabó significando «aguijón».

## Fuentes

- Jim Kaler, Stars, Lesath (stars.astro.illinois.edu/sow/lesath.html): «Lesath ... is intimately related in the observer's eye to Shaula (Lambda Scorpii), the two making the Scorpion's famed, and very real-looking, stinger»; «they do not constitute a double»; «The name originally came from a Greek word that means "foggy," and according to recent scholarship most likely refers to the fuzzy objects of the Milky Way, notably to the naked-eye cluster Messier 7, which is nearby ... The name was then translated into Arabic, badly re-translated to Latin, and mistranslated back into Arabic to a word that meant "sting," Lesath's origin a mismatch of three languages».
