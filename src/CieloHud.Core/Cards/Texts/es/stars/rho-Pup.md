# Tureis

Su nombre viene de un «pequeño escudo» del Argo, el barco de Jasón. Es una de las estrellas más brillantes de un tipo que
late deprisa y con mucha regularidad: su brillo sube y baja un 10 % cada 3 horas, 22 minutos y 52 segundos.

## Fuentes

- Jim Kaler, Stars, Tureis (stars.astro.illinois.edu/sow/tureis.html): «Within the great ship Argo, in which Jason sailed to find the golden fleece, is a group of stars that represents a "little shield." The term was ... then in Arabic to the star we now know as Rho Puppis»; «Tureis ... makes its mark as one of the sky's brightest "Delta Scuti" variables»; «they pulsate subtly and quickly. Tureis changes by only about 10% over a precisely known period of 0.14088143 days (3 hours 22 minutes 52 seconds)».
