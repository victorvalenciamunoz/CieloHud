# Merak

La otra estrella de «los punteros» del Carro, que con Dubhe señala hacia la Estrella Polar. Su nombre árabe significa
«el costado de la Osa Mayor». Como Vega, la rodea un disco de polvo, del tamaño de la órbita de Saturno, parecido al que
formó nuestros planetas. Es una de las cinco estrellas centrales del Carro, que viajan juntas por el espacio.

## Fuentes

- Jim Kaler, Stars, Merak (stars.astro.illinois.edu/sow/merak.html): «Dubhe at the lip of the Dipper's bowl and Merak, also at the bowl's front ..., the two making the Big Dipper's "Pointers" that lead the way to the North Star»; «"Merak" coming from an Arabic description that means "the flank of the Greater Bear"»; «it is a Vega kind of star, one that radiates extra infrared light that seems to be coming from a disk-like shroud of heated dust, one reminiscent of the dusty disk that produced our planets. Merak's detected disk approaches the orbit of Saturn in size»; «Merak is also a prominent part of the Ursa Major Cluster, as are all the Dipper's stars but the two at the ends».
