# Suhail

Los árabes llamaban «Suhail» a varias estrellas del sur, entre ellas Canopo, y al final el nombre se quedó solo en esta;
nadie sabe qué significa. Es una supergigante tan grande que en el lugar del Sol llegaría casi a la órbita de la Tierra, y
su brillo cambia un poco, sin un ritmo fijo.

## Fuentes

- Jim Kaler, Stars, Suhail (stars.astro.illinois.edu/sow/suhail.html): «The ancient Arabs used "Suhail" as a prefix for the names of a number of southern stars, including Canopus and Regor ... the name was eventually transferred exclusively to the star is now called "Lamda."»; «this orange class K supergiant (K4, and perhaps more properly classified as a "bright giant"), is also an "irregular variable" that wobbles erratically»; «a radius 207 times that of the Sun, which is close to the size of the Earth's orbit».
- Jim Kaler, Stars, Regor (stars.astro.illinois.edu/sow/regor.html): «Unfortunately, after all this, nobody seems to know what "Suhail" actually means!».
