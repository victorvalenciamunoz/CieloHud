# Delta de Perseo

Una gigante caliente que probablemente forma parte, con Mirfak, del cúmulo de Alfa Persei. Tiene unos 50 millones de años
y acaba de agotar el hidrógeno de su centro: va camino de convertirse en una gigante roja. Gira muy deprisa, y su día dura
como mucho dos de los nuestros.

## Fuentes

- Jim Kaler, Stars, Delta Per (stars.astro.illinois.edu/sow/deltaper.html): «Delta Per is a hot, luminous mid-class B (B5) giant»; «Only 50 million years old (and consistent with its "giant" status), it recently gave up hydrogen fusion in its now-helium core, and is making the transition to becoming a swollen red giant»; «it is most likely a member of the sprawling "Alpha Persei cluster"»; «madly rotating with an equatorial speed of at least 255 kilometers per second, giving it a "day" of at most two of our Earth days».
