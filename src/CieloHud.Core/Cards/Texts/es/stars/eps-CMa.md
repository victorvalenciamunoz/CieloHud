# Adhara

La segunda estrella más brillante del Can Mayor, en sus patas traseras. Su nombre viene de «las vírgenes», el nombre árabe
de un pequeño grupo de tres estrellas del que ella es la del oeste. Es tan caliente que buena parte de su luz es
ultravioleta: si nuestros ojos vieran esa luz, Adhara sería la estrella más brillante del cielo.

## Fuentes

- Jim Kaler, Stars, Adhara (stars.astro.illinois.edu/sow/adhara.html): «it's actually the second brightest star in its constellation (after Sirius), and helps form the western leg of Canis Major»; «The ancient Arabs referred to this small three-star pattern as "The Virgins," to which the name "Adhara" actually refers (Adhara the westernmost of the three)»; «Because of the star's high temperature, it radiates a good portion of its energy in the invisible ultraviolet»; «Indeed, if you had ultraviolet eyes, Adhara would be the brightest star in the sky».
- Jim Kaler, Stars, Mirzam (stars.astro.illinois.edu/sow/mirzam.html): «Delta, Epsilon (first magnitude Adhara), and Zeta are lower down, marking the Dog's hindquarters and rear legs».
