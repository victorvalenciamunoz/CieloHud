# Vindemiatrix

Su nombre latino significa «la vendimiadora»: para los griegos, verla aparecer por primera vez con la luz del amanecer
indicaba que era hora de recoger la uva. Es una gigante con mucha actividad magnética: emite casi 300 veces más rayos X que
el Sol.

## Fuentes

- Jim Kaler, Stars, Vindemiatrix (stars.astro.illinois.edu/sow/vindemiatrix.html): «The name is a somewhat corrupted feminized Latin form for the original Greek name that meant "the Grape Gatherer," as the first visibility of the star in morning light after the Sun cleared out of the way (the "heliacal rising") told that it was time to pick the grapes»; «yellow class G (G8) giant»; «Vindemiatrix is a rather powerful X-ray source, implying considerable magnetic surface activity. Its total X-ray radiation is almost 300 times that of the Sun».
