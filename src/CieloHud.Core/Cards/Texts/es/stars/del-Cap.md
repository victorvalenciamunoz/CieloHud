# Deneb Algedi

Su nombre árabe significa «la cola del cabrito», la de Capricornio. Una compañera la eclipsa cada 1,023 días, y su brillo baja
lo justo para notarlo a simple vista si se mira con cuidado. Es de las estrellas más brillantes de un tipo con mucho metal en
su superficie, porque en su atmósfera unos elementos se hunden y otros suben.

## Fuentes

- Jim Kaler, Stars, Deneb Algedi (stars.astro.illinois.edu/sow/denebalgedi.html): «"Deneb" in Arabic refers to "tail," ... and here the "Kid's tail."»; «a companion of unknown type that eclipses it every 1.023 days, causing the apparent brightness to drop by about 0.2 magnitude, just enough to be seen (with care) with the naked eye»; «Its greatest claim to distinction is that it is among the brightest of the "metallic A stars," those hotter stars that seem to be highly enriched in most metals»; «More likely, some process has separated various chemical elements, causing some to drift downward, others to rise upward».
