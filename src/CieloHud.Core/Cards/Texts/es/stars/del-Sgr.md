# Kaus Media

«El arco del medio»: está en el centro del arco de Sagitario, donde empieza la flecha, que apunta hacia el corazón de la Vía
Láctea. Es una gigante tan grande que en el lugar del Sol llegaría a tres cuartas partes de la órbita de Mercurio.

## Fuentes

- Jim Kaler, Stars, Kaus Media (stars.astro.illinois.edu/sow/kausmed.html): «an arrow along with it that points into the heart of the Milky Way. At the bow's northern end lies Kaus Borealis, at the southern Kaus Australis, and in the middle and marking the eastern end of the arrow Kaus Media, the names an odd mixture of languages, Kaus coming from an Arabic word meaning "bow," while the other three words respectively mean, from Latin, "northern," "southern," and "middle."»; «a radius 62 times solar, the star three-quarters the size of Mercury's orbit».
