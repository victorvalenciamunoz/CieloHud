# Phact

La principal de la Paloma, una constelación moderna que representa la paloma de Noé. Su nombre árabe significa «la
paloma». Gira tan deprisa, al menos 90 veces más que el Sol, que se achata por los polos y suelta a su alrededor
una capa de gas.

## Fuentes

- Jim Kaler, Stars, Phact (stars.astro.illinois.edu/sow/phact.html): «Columba, the Dove. A modern constellation carved in the seventeenth century from the western outlying stars of Argo, Jason's ship, Columba was meant to represent Noah's Dove»; «The star's name comes directly from Arabic, and means "the Ring Dove."» (una paloma); «spinning rapidly, at a speed of at least 180 kilometers per second at its equator (90 times that of the Sun)»; «The rapid rotation causes the star to flatten at its poles and to spin off a low density envelope about twice its radius».
- Ian Ridpath, Star Tales, Columba (ianridpath.com/startales/columba.html): «A constellation formed in the late 16th century by the Dutch cartographer and astronomer Petrus Plancius». Kaler dice siglo XVII y Ridpath finales del XVI: el texto dice «moderna», sin siglo.
