# Fomalhaut

Su nombre árabe significa «la boca del pez austral»: es la principal del Pez Austral, en una zona con pocas estrellas
brillantes, y por eso destaca tan sola. La rodea un enorme disco de polvo helado. En 2008 se anunció un planeta dentro
de él, pero el Hubble mostró después que era una nube de polvo: los restos de un choque entre dos cuerpos de unos 200 km.

## Fuentes

- Jim Kaler, Stars, Fomalhaut (stars.astro.illinois.edu/sow/fomalhaut.html): «the name comes from an Arabic phrase meaning "the mouth of the southern fish"»; «marks for us the otherwise dim constellation Piscis Austrinus»; «slides slowly in lonely grandeur»; «a huge disk of matter five times the size of the orbit of Pluto»; «The disk is thought to be made of icy dust particles».
- ESA/Hubble, heic2006 (esahubble.org/news/heic2006): «The object was previously believed to be a planet, called Fomalhaut b, and was first announced in 2008»; «Fomalhaut b is not a planet, but a slowly expanding cloud blasted into space as a result of a collision between two large bodies»; «each of these comet-like bodies measured about 200 kilometers across».
