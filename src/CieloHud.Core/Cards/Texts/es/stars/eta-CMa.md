# Aludra

Su nombre árabe significa «la virginidad»: forma parte de «las vírgenes», el grupo de estrellas de la parte trasera del Can
Mayor, con Adhara y Wezen. Es una supergigante con un viento tan fuerte que ya ha perdido el equivalente a un tercio del
Sol. Su distancia es difícil de medir directamente.

## Fuentes

- Jim Kaler, Stars, Aludra (stars.astro.illinois.edu/sow/aludra.html): «The name comes from an Arabic word that means "the virginity," and alludes to the Arabic group called "the Virgins," which consists of the stars of the Dog's lower portions (and which include Adhara and Wezen)»; «Like its mate Wezen, Aludra is a bright supergiant»; «Its distance ..., however, presents a problem. Direct measures of parallax ... by the Hipparcos satellite gives 3200 light years, but with a very high potential error»; «Aludra has a strong wind ... it has already lost about a third the mass of the whole Sun».
- Hipparcos (van Leeuwen 2007, VizieR I/311): la paralaje de Aludra tiene un error de más del 20 %, y la app da un intervalo (decisión 044).
