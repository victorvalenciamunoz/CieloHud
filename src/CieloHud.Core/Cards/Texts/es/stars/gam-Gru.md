# Aldhanab

Su nombre árabe significa «la cola», la del Pez Austral, aunque en la figura de la Grulla está en la cabeza. Es una de tantas
estrellas «Deneb», la palabra árabe para «cola», como Deneb del Cisne, Denebola o Deneb Algedi. Es una gigante que empieza a
hincharse y llegará a brillar unas 20 veces más.

## Fuentes

- Jim Kaler, Stars, Al Dhanab (stars.astro.illinois.edu/sow/aldhanab.html): «To the ancient Arabians, the stars of the Crane were part of the tail of ancient Piscis Austrinus, the Southern Fish ... the name of Gamma, Al Dhanab, referring to the fish's tail itself. The anglicized "Deneb" and its variant "Dhanab" literally mean "tail" out of Arabic. And many there are, starting with the best-known, Deneb in Cygnus. Then follow Denebola in Leo, Deneb Kaitos in Cetus, Deneb Algedi in Capricornus ... and now the last of them, our al Dhanab, which in the head of the Crane is quite misplaced»; «a solitary ... blue-white class B (B8) giant»; «the red-giant-to-be will first reach a luminosity some 20 times what it is today».
