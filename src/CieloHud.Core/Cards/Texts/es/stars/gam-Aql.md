# Tarazed

Su nombre viene del persa y significa «el astil de la balanza»: Altair y sus dos vecinas, Tarazed y Alshain, parecían una
balanza. Es una gigante tan grande que, en el lugar del Sol, llegaría a medio camino de la Tierra, y la veríamos ocupar dos
tercios de la altura del cielo, del horizonte al cénit.

## Fuentes

- Jim Kaler, Stars, Tarazed (stars.astro.illinois.edu/sow/tarazed.html): «"Tarazed" comes to us from a Persian phrase meaning "the Beam of the Scale," and was originally applied to the star Altair and its two flanking stars, bright third magnitude (2.72) Tarazed and fourth magnitude Alshain, as together they look like a weighing balance»; «a class K (K3) bright giant»; «if the star were our Sun it would extend halfway to the orbit of the Earth and appear 60 degrees across in our sky, two-thirds of the way from the horizon to the point overhead».
