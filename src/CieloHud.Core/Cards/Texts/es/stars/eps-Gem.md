# Mebsuta

Para los árabes, esta estrella y su vecina Mekbuda eran las patas de un león: Mebsuta, la pata estirada, y Mekbuda, la
doblada. Es una supergigante tan grande que ocuparía la órbita de Venus. Como está en el camino de la Luna, se ha medido su
tamaño cronometrando cuánto tarda la Luna en taparla.

## Fuentes

- Jim Kaler, Stars, Mebsuta (stars.astro.illinois.edu/sow/mebsuta.html): «In ancient Arabic lore the two stars were the paws of a lion ... Mekbuda comes from a phrase meaning the lion's folded paw, while Mebsuta refers to the outstretched paw»; «a cool class G supergiant»; «a diameter 150 times that of the Sun, or about the size of the orbit of Venus»; «Mebsuta is one of the few supergiants that lie along the path of the Moon. By timing how long it takes the moving Moon to cover, or occult, a star, we can measure the star's angular diameter».
