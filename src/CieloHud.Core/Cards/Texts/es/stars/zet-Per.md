# Zeta de Perseo

La tercera estrella más brillante de Perseo, aunque lleve la sexta letra griega. Es una supergigante muy caliente y joven,
de unos 9 millones de años, que acabará estallando como supernova. Encabeza un grupo de estrellas jóvenes y calientes que
nacieron juntas y se van separando, y que lleva su nombre.

## Fuentes

- Jim Kaler, Stars, Zeta Per (stars.astro.illinois.edu/sow/zetaper.html): «though the third magnitude (2.85 and slightly variable) star is sixth in order of the Greek alphabet, it is number three in Perseus's brightness parade»; «loose gangs of hot blue stars called "OB associations" ... that, unlike gravitationally bound clusters, are quickly disintegrating, their stars moving away from each other»; «the "Perseus OB2" association, so markedly features Zeta Persei that the group is also known as the "Zeta Per Association."»; «an ultraluminous hot (23,000 Kelvin) class B (B1) supergiant»; «As a 19 solar mass supergiant only nine million years old»; «Its only fate is to explode as a supernova».
