# Gamma 2 de la Vela

Su apodo, Regor, es «Roger» al revés: un homenaje a Roger Chaffee, uno de los astronautas que murieron en el incendio del
Apolo. Son dos estrellas muy calientes que giran juntas cada 78,5 días. Una es la Wolf-Rayet más brillante del cielo, un
tipo raro de estrella moribunda que ha perdido sus capas exteriores. Las dos acabarán estallando como supernovas.

## Fuentes

- Jim Kaler, Stars, Regor (stars.astro.illinois.edu/sow/regor.html): «Regor, however, is "Roger" spelled backwards, and honors Roger Chaffee, one of the astronauts who died in the Apollo fire»; «it is actually a pair of stars ... The brighter of the two is a hot hydrogen-fusing dwarf of class O ..., while the other is the visually brightest "Wolf-Rayet" star in the sky, the two in 78.5 day orbit»; «Wolf-Rayet stars ... are very rare and in an extremely advanced state of age ... They have stripped off most of their mass, their outer hydrogen envelopes»; «almost certainly in the last stages of preparing to blow up as a supernova. The O star will follow much the same path and go supernova as well».
