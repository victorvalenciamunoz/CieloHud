# Hamal

Su nombre árabe significa «el cordero», por toda la constelación de Aries. Hace más de 2000 años, el punto donde el Sol cruza
el ecuador del cielo en marzo estaba en Aries; en tiempos de Homero, a solo 9° de Hamal. Su tamaño se ha medido con tanta
precisión que se ha visto que su borde es más oscuro que el centro, como pasa con el Sol.

## Fuentes

- Jim Kaler, Stars, Hamal (stars.astro.illinois.edu/sow/hamal.html): «The name, directly from Arabic, means "the lamb," rather obviously standing in for the whole constellation of the Ram»; «The vernal equinox, the point, where the solar path crosses the celestial equator, is in Pisces ... Over 2000 years ago, however, it was in Aries»; «Around the time of Homer, the equinox was situated only nine degrees south of Hamal»; «It has the honor of having (along with Shedar, Alpha Cassiopeiae) the most accurately-measured angular diameter ... This precise measurement allowed the detection of Hamal's "limb darkening." As a gaseous sphere, the Sun is slightly darker at the edge ... than at the center».
