# El cometa Shoemaker-Levy 9 choca con Júpiter

Empiezan a caer en Júpiter unos 21 trozos del cometa Shoemaker-Levy 9, durante seis días. Es el primer choque entre dos cuerpos del sistema solar que se observa, y deja grandes manchas oscuras.

## Fuentes

- NASA Science, Comet Shoemaker-Levy 9 (science.nasa.gov/solar-system/comets/p-shoemaker-levy-9): «The impacts started on July 16, 1994, and ended on July 22, 1994».
- NASA Science, The Lasting Impacts of Comet Shoemaker-Levy 9 (science.nasa.gov/science-research/planetary-science/the-lasting-impacts-comet-shoemaker-levy-9): «The fragments – some 21 in all – plunged into Jupiter's atmosphere over the course of six days».
- NASA SVS, Hubble Space Telescope Observes the Comet P/Shoemaker-Levy 9 Collision with Jupiter (svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/3636): «This is the first collision of two solar system bodies ever to be observed».
- NASA, How Historic Jupiter Comet Impact Led to Planetary Defense (nasa.gov/centers-and-facilities/goddard/how-historic-jupiter-comet-impact-led-to-planetary-defense): «creating huge, dark scars in the planet's atmosphere».
- NASA Science, Comet Shoemaker-Levy's First Fragment Impacts With Jupiter (science.nasa.gov/asset/hubble/comet-shoemaker-levys-first-fragment-impacts-with-jupiter): «taken at 5:32 EDT on July 16, 1994, shortly after the impact of the first fragment (A)»; «1.5 hours after the impact». Deducido: el primer choque fue hacia las 4:00 EDT del 16, que son las 8:00 o las 20:00 UT del mismo día; en UTC también es el 16.
