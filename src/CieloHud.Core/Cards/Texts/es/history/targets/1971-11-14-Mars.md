# Mariner 9, la primera en órbita de otro planeta

La sonda Mariner 9 entra en órbita de Marte, la primera alrededor de otro planeta. Llega en plena tormenta de polvo global; cuando amaina, ve de cerca por primera vez sus volcanes.

## Fuentes

- NASA Science, Mariner 9 (science.nasa.gov/mission/mariner-9): «At 00:18 UT Nov. 14, 1971, Mariner 9 ignited its main engine for 915.6 seconds to become the first human-made object to enter orbit around another planet».
- NASA NSSDCA, Mariner 9 (nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/nmc/spacecraft/display.action?id=1971-051A): «Mariner 9 arrived at Mars on 14 November 1971»; «making Mariner 9 the first spacecraft to orbit another planet»; «The storm quickly grew into one of the largest global storms ever observed on Mars»; «By the time the spacecraft arrived at Mars no surface details could be seen except the summits of Olympus Mons and the three Tharsis volcanoes»; «The storm abated through November and December»; «the first detailed views of the martian volcanoes».
