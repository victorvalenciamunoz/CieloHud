# Venera 9, la primera foto del suelo de Venus

La sonda Venera 9 se posa en Venus y aguanta 53 minutos, lo justo para enviar la primera foto de su suelo: rocas de 30 a 40 cm, en parte enterradas.

## Fuentes

- NASA Science, First Look: Venus (science.nasa.gov/resource/first-look-venus): «The lander touched down at 5:13 UT (with the sun near zenith) on 22 October 1975 and operated for 53 minutes, allowing return of this single image»; «Angular and partly weathered rocks, about 30 to 40 cm across, dominate the landscape, many partly buried in soil».
- ESA, The first images of the surface of Venus (esa.int/ESA_Multimedia/Images/2005/08/The_first_images_of_the_surface_of_Venus): «The first images of the surface of Venus were returned by the Soviet Union's Venera 9 and 10 spacecraft on 22 and 25 October 1975». NASA dice «one of the first»; ESA concreta que la del 22 es la de Venera 9.
