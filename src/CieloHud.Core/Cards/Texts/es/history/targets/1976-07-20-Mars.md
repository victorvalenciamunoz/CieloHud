# Viking 1 se posa en Marte

La sonda Viking 1 se posa en Marte, la primera que lo consigue y sigue funcionando. Segundos después empieza a enviar la primera foto desde su suelo, con una de sus patas.

## Fuentes

- NASA Science, Viking 1 (science.nasa.gov/mission/viking-1): «NASA's Viking 1 made the first truly successful landing on Mars»; «The Soviet Mars 3 lander claimed a technical first with a survivable landing in 1971, but contact was lost seconds after it touched down»; «Viking Lander 1 set down safely […] at 11:53:06 UT July 20, 1976».
- NASA NSSDCA, Viking 1 Lander (nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/nmc/spacecraft/display.action?id=1975-075C): «The Viking 1 Lander touched down in western Chryse Planitia […] at 11:53:06 UT»; «Transmission of the first surface image began 25 seconds after landing».
- NASA, 45 years ago: Viking 1 Touches Down on Mars (nasa.gov/history/45-years-ago-viking-1-touches-down-on-mars): «The first photograph ever returned from the surface of Mars, by the Viking 1 lander, including one of its footpads». La misma página dice «the first U.S. spacecraft to land on another planet», un hito de un país que el texto no usa.
