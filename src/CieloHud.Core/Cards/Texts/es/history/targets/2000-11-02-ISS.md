# Llega la Expedición 1

Llega la Expedición 1, los tres primeros tripulantes que viven en la estación. Con ellos empieza su ocupación sin interrupción.

## Fuentes

- NASA, Looking Back: International Space Station Start of Expedition 1 (nasa.gov/image-article/looking-back-international-space-station-start-of-expedition-1): «On Nov. 2, 2000, the Expedition 1 crew – Commander William M. (Bill) Shepherd of NASA and Flight Engineer Sergei Krikalev and Soyuz Commander Yuri Gidzenko of Roscosmos – arrived at the International Space Station, marking the start of an uninterrupted human presence on the orbiting laboratory»; acoplamiento «at 3:21 a.m. CST» (09:21 UT); «the hatch leading into the Zvezda's living quarters was opened, signifying the start of human occupancy».
- NASA, Space Station Facts and Figures (nasa.gov/international-space-station/space-station-facts-and-figures): «continuously occupied since November 2000». Sale del texto de la ficha de la ISS (decisión 051).
