# Pioneer 11, la primera visita a Saturno

La sonda Pioneer 11 pasa junto a Saturno, la primera que lo visita. Descubre un anillo nuevo y estrecho, el F.

## Fuentes

- NASA Science, Pioneer 11 (science.nasa.gov/mission/pioneer-11): «the first human-made object to fly past Saturn»; «The spacecraft crossed the planet's ring plane beyond the outer ring at 14:36 UT Sept. 1, 1979, and then passed by the planet at 16:31 UT»; «Among Pioneer 11's many discoveries were a narrow ring outside the A ring named the F ring».
- NASA NSSDCA, Pioneer 11 (nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/nmc/spacecraft/display.action?id=1973-019A): «the first to explore the planet Saturn and its main rings»; «It passed by Saturn on 01 September 1979».
