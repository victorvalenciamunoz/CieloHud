# Mars Pathfinder y Sojourner llegan a Marte

La sonda Mars Pathfinder se posa en Marte rebotando dentro de unas bolsas de aire. Lleva a Sojourner, que dos días después es el primer vehículo con ruedas en otro planeta.

## Fuentes

- NASA NSSDCA, Mars Pathfinder (nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/nmc/spacecraft/display.action?id=1996-068A): «four air bags inflated in about 0.3 seconds forming a 5.2 meter diameter protective 'ball' around the lander»; «impacted at 16:56:55 UT (12:56:55 p.m. EDT) on 4 July 1997»; «bounced about 12 meters (40 feet) into the air, bouncing at least another 15 times»; «the rover […] rolled onto the surface on 6 July at about 05:40 UT».
- NASA, 25 Years Ago: Mars Pathfinder Launches to Mars to Deploy Sojourner, the First Planetary Rover (nasa.gov/history/25-years-ago-mars-pathfinder-launches-to-mars-to-deploy-sojourner-the-first-planetary-rover): «The landing of Mars Pathfinder and its Sojourner rover on July 4, 1997»; «Sojourner "stood up" on its six wheels and backed down one of the lander's two ramps onto the surface, becoming the first wheeled vehicle operated on another planet».
