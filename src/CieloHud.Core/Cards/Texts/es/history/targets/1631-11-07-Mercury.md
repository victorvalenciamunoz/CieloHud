# Gassendi ve el primer tránsito de Mercurio

Pierre Gassendi ve con su telescopio a Mercurio cruzar por delante del Sol: es el primer tránsito confirmado. Lo había predicho Kepler, que murió sin verlo.

## Fuentes

- NASA, Sun-Earth Day 2007, copia archivada por NASA (spdf.gsfc.nasa.gov/pub/documents/old/websites/sunearthday.nasa.gov/2007/locations/ttt_transit.php): «The first confirmed transit of Mercury occurred on November 7, 1631 and was sighted telescopically by the French astronomer Pierre Gassendi (1592-1655). This transit had been predicted by Johannes Kepler (1571-1630), however, Kepler never lived to see this vindication».
- NASA, Catalog of Transits of Mercury (eclipse.gsfc.nasa.gov/transit/catalog/MercuryCatalog.html): «1631 Nov 07»; «The Gregorian calendar is used for all dates beginning 1582 Oct 15». Fecha gregoriana, la que ya usaba Francia; no la juliana.
