# Luna 3, la primera foto de la cara oculta

La sonda Luna 3 hace las primeras fotos de la cara oculta de la Luna: 29 imágenes borrosas del 70 % de ella. Sorprende lo que falta: no tiene los grandes mares oscuros de la cara que vemos.

## Fuentes

- NASA NSSDCA, Earth's Moon – Luna 3 (nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/imgcat/html/object_page/lu3_2.html): «The first image was taken at 03:30 UT on 7 October at a distance of 63,500 km after Luna 3 had passed the Moon and looked back at the sunlit far side»; «A total of 29 photographs were taken, covering 70% of the far side»; «The photographs were very noisy and of low resolution».
- NASA Science, First Photo of the Lunar Far Side (science.nasa.gov/resource/first-photo-of-the-lunar-far-side): «In October of 1959, the Luna 3 spacecraft launched from the Baikonur Cosmodrome in Kazakhstan»; «because the Moon always presents the same face to the Earth, they offered views of a part of the Moon that had never been seen before»; «The most striking difference evident in the Luna 3 pictures is the absence of the large, dark seas of cooled lava, called maria, that cover a substantial fraction of the Earth-facing near side».
