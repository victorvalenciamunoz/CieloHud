# El Apolo 8, los primeros en dar vueltas a la Luna

El Apolo 8 entra en órbita de la Luna: Borman, Lovell y Anders son los primeros en dar vueltas a otro mundo. Anders fotografía la Tierra asomando por el horizonte lunar.

## Fuentes

- NASA NSSDCA, Apollo 8 (nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/nmc/spacecraft/display.action?id=1968-118A): «The spacecraft was launched on December 21, 1968»; «Orbit insertion took place on 24 December at 09:59:20 UT».
- NASA, Apollo 8: Christmas at the Moon (nasa.gov/history/apollo-8-christmas-at-the-moon): «the Apollo 8 astronauts—Frank Borman, Jim Lovell and Bill Anders – became the first humans to orbit another world».
- NASA, This Week in NASA History: Apollo 8 Captures "Earthrise" – Dec. 24, 1968 (nasa.gov/centers/marshall/history/this-week-in-nasa-history-apollo-8-captures-earthrise-dec-24-1968.html): «this iconic "Earthrise" photograph, snapped by Anders»; «The photo shows Earth rising over the horizon of the Moon».
