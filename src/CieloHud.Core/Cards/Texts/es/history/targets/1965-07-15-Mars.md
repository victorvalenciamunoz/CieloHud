# Mariner 4, las primeras fotos de cerca de Marte

La sonda Mariner 4 pasa junto a Marte y hace las primeras fotos de cerca de otro planeta. Muestran un mundo desierto y lleno de cráteres.

## Fuentes

- NASA NSSDCA, Mariner 4 (nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/nmc/spacecraft/display.action?id=1964-077A): «the first successful flyby of the planet Mars, returning the first pictures of the martian surface»; «These represented the first images of another planet ever returned from deep space»; «The camera sequence started at 00:18:36 UT on July 15 (7:18:49 p.m. EST on July 14)»; «The closest approach was 9,846 km from the Martian surface at 01:00:57 UT 15 July 1965 (8:00:57 p.m. EST 14 July)». En EE. UU. aún era el 14.
- NASA Science, Mariner 4 (science.nasa.gov/mission/mariner-4): «views of a barren, heavily cratered planet»; «the first close-up images of another planet».
