# La primera foto de una estrella

William Cranch Bond y John Adams Whipple fotografían a Vega desde el observatorio de Harvard. Es la primera foto de una estrella, aparte del Sol.

## Fuentes

- ESA, 17 July (esa.int/Science_Exploration/Space_Science/17_July): «On 17 July 1850, the Harvard Observatory in the United States took the first photograph of a star. The observatory director, W.C. Bond and a Boston photographer J.A. Whipple took a daguerreotype of Vega».
- ESA Science & Technology, A history of astrometry - Part II (sci.esa.int/web/gaia/-/53197-seeing-and-measuring-farther, leída en su copia de web.archive.org: la página da un error de certificado): «The first photographs of the Moon and Sun appeared in the 1840s and the first photographic image of a star - Vega - was obtained by American astronomers William Cranch Bond and John Adams Whipple in 1850».
